**A tidier tray, a license that follows you instead of the PC, and Today and Widgets that come alive.** The tray menu is cut down to the essentials, "In full-screen apps" and the game options move into Advanced settings, and a Halo Pro license now works as one seat at a time — whichever PC uses it last keeps it. Today and Widgets are redrawn with a soft, springy entrance on every card.

- **The tray menu is shorter.** Eight rarely-used entries are gone from the hidden-icon menu (Theme, Idle look, Halo Mascot, Synced lyrics, Apple Music lyrics, Start with Windows, Open data folder, Lyrics by LRCLIB). The essentials stay: Advanced settings, Keep island open, Sounds, What's New, About, Halo Pro and Quit.
- **"In full-screen apps" lives in Advanced settings now.** The game-mode rows (hide the island in full-screen apps, game stats and peek) move out of the tray into Advanced › Island, where they sit with the other full-screen controls. The readout stays off without Halo Pro.
- **"Every setting" is gone.** Advanced settings no longer lists an "Every setting" view; each section opens on its own.
- **A license is one seat, not one PC.** A Halo Pro license is no longer tied to a fixed PC or a device id. It is time-based — a trial that ends on a date, or permanent. One PC uses it at a time: when a second PC activates the same license, the first PC loses Pro on its next check (the island says its seat was moved). Uninstalling Halo Island keeps the license, so a reinstall on the same seat is Pro again without a new code. While a server license is on, the app asks the license server once a day — skipping while the PC is locked, the display is off or battery saver is on — so a moved or revoked seat drops promptly. The admin portal can now show a license key after it is created.
- **Today and Widgets, redrawn.** Today gets an accent rule under the date, the weekend columns and this week's band, a date disc whose halo breathes, a feed dot that pings, and stories that rise in as cards with a staggered accent bar, a hover outline, a press dip and a smooth scroll. Widgets gets tiles with a colour glow and a light sweep, a drawn picture on each tile where the words leave room (weather, clock, calendar, timer, moon), and a water card with the water inside the ring, a ripple and spring on −/+, and an outline once the goal is reached.

**What it costs:** nothing new runs at rest. Opening Today or Widgets asks for frames (~30 fps) for about ten seconds while the ambient motion settles, and none under reduced motion or battery saver. A hover or tap asks for frames for under a second. The daily license check reads a small answer once a day, only while a server license is on.

**Install:** download `Halo-Island-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Halo\halo.exe`.

**Upgrading:** run the new Setup over the old one. It upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons, sign-in and now your license too.

The Windows build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
