**Nine fixes and asks, in one release.** The Weather widget reads again, wherever the PC is. Stocks shows real prices, from Bursa Malaysia and any exchange in the world. The AI tab shows your whole Mireld balance. Halo Pro reads like an account, with a trial's time counting down. And the adzan has a second voice, with Subuh's own.

- **Weather that always has a place.** The widget no longer says "Can't reach it" when the trouble was the place. It reads for, in this order: a city you pick (click the Weather tile, type a city from Prayer times' list or the world's, or **Use my location**), Windows' location, the Prayer times place, then where your internet address says you are, from ipwho.is, once a run, labelled "Near … (by internet address)" since it's an estimate. With Windows' location off and nothing else, the tile says **Location off · click to pick a city**. The last reading is kept, after a restart too, and shows with its age while you're offline. Open-Meteo still gives the reading, keyless, with a 10 s timeout and a growing wait after a failure, and only while the Widgets tab is open. Google has no public weather API, so the widget doesn't ask Google.
- **Stocks with real prices.** The tab's feed address was a placeholder that was never deployed. Prices now come from Yahoo Finance, no key and no account: the FTSE Bursa Malaysia KLCI first, and a new list starts with Maybank, Public Bank, CIMB, Tenaga and IHH. **Add a stock** finds Bursa's 54 biggest companies at once (by name, short name or number: Maybank, MAYBANK, 1155), and anything Yahoo Finance lists worldwide once you stop typing (Apple, AAPL, 7203.T). Each price says how old it is on its exchange's clock. The tab asks every minute while one of your markets trades and every 10 minutes while they're all shut, only while it's open. When Yahoo asks it to slow down, it says **Market closed or limited**, not a spinner. A list from the IDX days keeps its stocks as their Jakarta symbols (BBCA.JK); one that was still the five starters gets Bursa's.
- **Your whole Mireld balance.** With a Mireld key, the AI tab asks `api.mireld.my/v1/usage` too, and the card shows everything it says: the plan, tokens used of the total as a bar and in full (6,474,910,074 of 7,375,000,000, 87.8%), tokens left, available and reserved, when the credits expire as a countdown with the date, the key's prefix and state, the request count, the per-minute and at-once limits, and why there's no history. Where `/v1/usage` has no balance, `/v1/credits` stands in. The key still goes to `api.mireld.my` and nowhere else.
- **Add-ons that say what's wrong.** A file that isn't on its release yet says "Its files aren't published yet", not a network error. `tools/publish_addons.py` gathers every add-on's files, checks each against the size and SHA-256 the app pins, and prints or runs the commands that publish them.
- **Every setting, in one list.** Advanced settings has a new section, **Every setting**: each item the window has, the section it lives in, and how it stands on this computer (its value, on or off, needs Halo Pro, needs an add-on, or not on this build). [docs/advanced-settings.md](https://github.com/kolijijani/halo-island/blob/main/docs/advanced-settings.md) is the same list, and a test keeps the two in step.
- **What's New, the big five first.** FPS over your games, Diorama, dropping files from a paired phone, Glass and Pill, and the Downloader, then the rest in a short block, so the sheet still fits without scrolling.
- **Halo Pro, like an account.** Settings › Halo Pro shows a badge that tells **TRIAL** (orange) from **PRO · PERMANENT** and **PRO** (yellow) at a glance, then the plan, when it ends ("ends in 23 days, 4 hours" and the date, counting down while the page is open), when it started, the license's id, this PC's id (the hash that's all that leaves it, with **Copy ID**) and what **Deactivate** does. The page asks no server: it reads the license this PC already keeps.
- **A choice of adzan voices.** Prayer times' Sound has **Adzan** (Aaqib Azeez), **Makkah** (the Maghrib adzan at Masjid al-Haram), **Tone** and **Silent**, with a play button to hear the voice first. Subuh now plays its own adzan, with "as-salatu khairum min an-naum" (Besim Azemi, Islamic Center Malmö), instead of the tone. Each voice is a file of its own on `addon-sholat-4`, downloaded only when you pick it (1.8 MB for Makkah, 2.4 MB for Subuh's) and deleted when you pick another; a voice that isn't on the PC plays the tone. Both are CC BY 3.0, credited in the add-on's notice. A saved choice loads as it was, and one this version doesn't know plays the tone.

**What it costs:** nothing new runs at rest. The Weather widget, Stocks and Halo Pro's countdown each work only while their tab or page is open; the countdown redraws on the minute the island already keeps. The voices download only when picked. halo.exe grows by the Bursa list and the new pages' code.

**Install:** download `Halo-Island-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Halo\halo.exe`.

**On a Mac:** open `Halo-Island-Mac.dmg`, drag Halo Island into Applications and open it.

**Upgrading:** the island says **Update available** within the hour; **Install now** in the Update sheet installs it for you. It upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and sign-in.

The Windows build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
