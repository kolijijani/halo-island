**Location and weather work again, Settings never hides, and Widgets becomes an add-on.**

- **Use my location finds you.** Windows' location question now comes from the right place, so Windows 11 asks for permission again instead of failing quietly. "Use my location" on Prayer times and on the Weather tile asks Windows afresh each time it's pressed, and a location that takes longer than 30 seconds says "timed out" instead of "unknown".
- **Weather reads again.** The Weather tile said "Can't reach it" even when the browser opened the weather service fine: Windows couldn't unpack the compressed answer it was sent. The weather is now asked for uncompressed. The tile also says why it has no reading: Offline, Timed out, Location off, Location timed out or Location unknown.
- **The pin and Settings always show.** With many tabs, the island's pin and Settings button keep their place. As the row fills, Halo's face, the date and the open tab's label give way first, then the tabs narrow, and the ones that still don't fit go behind a "…" menu. The tab you have open always stays in the row.
- **Widgets is an add-on.** The Widgets tab (Weather, Calendar, World clock, Moon, Countdown), Drink water and the clipboard list switch on and off together in Add-ons. It's off on new installs. If you upgrade with Drink water or the clipboard list on, Widgets stays on. Otherwise, switch it on in Add-ons. While it's off, the water timer, the clipboard listener and the weather reading all stop, and your clipboard list and last weather reading are removed. Your widget slots and water settings are kept for when you turn it back on.
- **A new Phone drop page.** The page your phone opens follows its light or dark mode, and you can pick Light, Dark or System yourself. It shows which PC you're connected to, its address and that nothing goes through the internet. Steps light up as a send moves from Choose to Accept on PC, Send and On the Shelf, and if a send fails, it shows the step where it stopped.

**What it costs:** nothing new runs at rest. With Widgets off, which is the default on new installs, less runs than before: no water timer, no clipboard listener and no weather reading. The weather is still asked for once every 30 minutes at most, now uncompressed, so each reading is about a kilobyte larger. The new Phone drop page adds a few kilobytes to halo.exe, and no new crate.

**Install:** download `Halo-Island-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Halo\halo.exe`.

**Upgrading:** run the new Setup over the old one. It upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons, sign-in and license.

The Windows build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
