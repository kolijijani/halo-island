**Games has its own tab.** Show over games moved out of the System page into a Games tab beside it, with new settings for what the readout over a game shows.

- **A Games tab in the notch.** It sits right after System and shows while the System add-on is on (Windows only). Settings on the left, the frame rate's last minute and frame times in the middle, and on the right the game in front: a ring of its frame rate between the GPU's load and temperature, the CPU, video memory and whether Windows lets Halo read frames (with the Allow FPS button).
- **Choose what the readout shows.** Switches for FPS, GPU temperature and CPU sit beside Stays/Peeks. The readout over a game narrows to what you leave on, in Classic and Pill.
- **Refresh rate.** Pick your screen's rate (60 to 360 Hz, or Auto) and the frame-rate chart and ring top out there.
- **The page draws in.** As Games opens, its ring and gauges draw to their readings and the chart runs out from the left. Nothing moves with reduced motion or battery saver on.
- **Every other tab, redrawn with motion.** System fills top to bottom and glides to the module you pick. Shelf, Tasks, Focus, AI, Script, Record, Prayer, Stocks, Caffeine, Downloads and Notifications have parts that rise and pop in as they open, hovers that fade in, and new touches: a dot on the Focus ring's tip, a reading mark in Script, a pulsing record dot, green badges for finished downloads, and a bell that rings once. Settings and Add-ons draw in row by row, with hovers that fade, Update cards that shine in and badges that brighten when switched on. Every switch slides and every picker glides to your choice. Music, Widgets and Today are as they were.

**What it costs:** nothing new runs at rest, and no new thread, timer or crate. A tab asks for frames only while it opens or answers you (about a second), plus 10 seconds of gentle motion on Focus while a session runs and on Record while it records, paused when the PC is locked or the screen is off. With reduced motion or battery saver on, nothing moves. The Games readings are the System page's own, once a second, as before.

**Install:** download `Halo-Island-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Halo\halo.exe`.

**Upgrading:** run the new Setup over the old one. It upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons, sign-in and license.

The Windows build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
