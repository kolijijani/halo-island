Prayer times add-on for Halo Island: what it holds and where it comes from

places.json
  Every location in Kemenag RI's (Bimas Islam) prayer schedule, named as
  Kemenag writes them, each with a reference point fitted to Kemenag's
  published 2026 schedule; plus Makkah, Madinah and Jeddah (coordinates
  from en.wikipedia.org). Part of Halo Island, under its MIT license.
