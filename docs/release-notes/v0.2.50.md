**A new face, an island that updates itself, and pages that come alive.** Halo Island wears the mascot's new cat icon, finds and installs new versions on its own, and every page now opens with a light, springy entrance. Water and Prayer times are redrawn, and the pop-ups bounce in.

- **Halo Island updates itself.** A couple of minutes after it starts, the app reads its signed update file, then looks again every half hour until a look gets through, at most once a day. **Check for updates…** in the menu or **Check now** in Settings looks at once. When a new version is out, it downloads the Setup, keeps it only if it matches the signed file to the byte, and says **Update available** on the island. Pick **Install now** (closes, updates and opens again) or **Install on next start**. Nothing installs until you choose, and the island says **Updated to** the new version afterwards. It doesn't look while the PC is locked, the display is off or battery saver is on.
- **A new cat icon.** Halo, the grey British Shorthair, with round ears and capsule eyes, from the approved design B. It stays sharp at every size Windows asks for, down to 16 px, and the installer wears it too.
- **Water and Prayer times, redrawn.** Water has a bigger ring that fills to today's share, "Water · 63%" (or "Goal reached"), the glass count in large type and round − and + buttons. Prayer times adds a bar under the countdown that shows how far you are from the last prayer to the next, and colours the part of the day already gone along the arc.
- **Every page opens with a little life.** As a page opens, its shapes arrive in turn: rings and bars draw to their value, cards rise, dots and buttons pop with a soft spring, all settled in about a second. Text and numbers stay put from the first frame, so nothing is harder to read. Done tasks sit on a faint green band. With Windows' animation effects off, or battery saver on, pages open still.
- **Pop-ups that bounce.** Caps Lock, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and charging pop in on a spring and settle as they go, with a short glow when something switches on (green for charging). The volume bar glides to its level instead of jumping, and a new notice lands with one soft glow.

**What it costs:** nothing new runs at rest. The entrance asks for frames for about a second as a page opens; a pop-up or a notice for about 0.7 s as it shows; then nothing until the next one. The update check reads a file of about 1 KB a couple of minutes after each start, then once every half hour only until a look gets through (at most once a day), and downloads the Setup (about 3 MB) once per release. The installer stays about 3 MB.

**Install:** download `Halo-Island-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Halo\halo.exe`.

**Upgrading:** 0.2.40 can't look for updates yet, so install this one by hand: run the new Setup over it. It upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons and sign-in. A 0.2.41 build finds this one on its own. From 0.2.50 on, the island tells you when a new version is out.

The Windows build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
