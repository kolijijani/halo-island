**An in-island tab popover, drag-to-reorder tabs, zero-expand gestures, Brightness and Microphone pop-ups, and searchable Advanced settings.**

**Contribution:** All development work for v0.2.55 was made by **Antigravity**, including features, bug fixes and testing.

- **In-island tab popover and reordering.** Clicking "…" on a crowded tab row now opens a rounded dark popover grid inside the island instead of a classic Win32 menu. Drag any tab horizontally in the header to reorder your favorite tabs to the front, or scroll your mouse wheel over the header to switch tabs. Reset to the default order any time in Advanced › Tabs.
- **Music page polish.** Album art gets a soft ambient glow tinted to the cover's accent color, and hovering or scrubbing the seek bar shows a floating timestamp capsule right over the cursor in both Classic and Pill themes.
- **Zero-expand gestures.** Middle-click the collapsed island while music plays to play or pause immediately without opening the island. Scroll your mouse wheel over the collapsed island to adjust system volume directly and show the Volume pop-up.
- **Brightness and Microphone pop-ups.** Changing display brightness shows a gliding sun-bar pop-up (via Windows power-setting notifications), and muting or unmuting your microphone shows a red/green Microphone pop-up (via Core Audio callbacks). Both can be switched on or off in Advanced › Pop-ups.
- **Settings search and pop-up previews.** Type in the new search box at the top of Advanced settings (or press Ctrl+F) to filter settings across all 13 sections, and click **Preview** beside any switch in Pop-ups to test its live pop-up on the island right away.

**What it costs:** nothing new runs at rest and no new thread, timer, poll or crate was added. Display brightness uses the existing `WM_POWERBROADCAST` window message, and microphone mute shares the existing `halo-audio` Core Audio callback thread (unbound completely when switched off).

**Install:** download `Halo-Island-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Halo\halo.exe`.

**Upgrading:** run the new Setup over the old one. It upgrades in place and keeps your settings, data, add-ons, sign-in and license.

The Windows build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
